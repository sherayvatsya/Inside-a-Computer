📖 What is Network?

A network is a system in which multiple devices, such as computers, smartphones, or servers, are connected to share data and resources. These connections can be wired, like cables, or wireless, like Wi-Fi. Networks allow communication between devices, enabling activities such as sending emails, browsing the internet, sharing files, and accessing remote services. The most common example is the Internet, which connects millions of devices worldwide. Networks can be small, like a home network, or large, like corporate or global networks. They improve efficiency, communication, and resource sharing in everyday digital life.

⚙️ Working of Network

The working of a network explains how devices communicate and share data efficiently. First, devices such as computers, smartphones, and servers are connected through wired (cables) or wireless (Wi-Fi) connections. When a user sends a request, like opening a website, the device converts the data into small units called packets. These packets contain the destination address and are sent across the network. Network devices such as routers and switches guide these packets through the best possible paths. Each packet may travel through different routes to reach its destination. At the receiving end, the device collects and reassembles all packets in the correct order to form the original data. Protocols like TCP/IP and HTTP control this entire process, ensuring accurate and secure communication over the Internet. Overall, networking enables fast, reliable, and efficient data exchange between devices worldwide.

🎮 Types of Network

LAN (Local Area Network)

A LAN connects devices within a small area like a home, school, or office. It offers high speed and is commonly used for sharing files, printers, and internet connections locally.

MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

A MAN covers a city or large campus. It connects multiple LANs together and provides high-speed connectivity across a wider area than LAN.

⚔️ Wired Network vs Wireless Network

🔌 Wired Network

Type: Uses physical cables (Ethernet)

Speed: Very Fast ⚡

Stability: Highly stable 📶

Mobility: Limited 🚫

Security: More secure 🔒

Setup: Requires cables & ports 🧩

Usage: Offices, Gaming, Servers

📡 Wireless Network

Type: Uses Wi-Fi / radio signals

Speed: Moderate 🚀

Stability: Can fluctuate 📡

Mobility: High 📱

Security: Less secure (needs protection) 🔐

Setup: Easy, no cables needed ⚙️

Usage: Homes, Cafes, Mobile devices

A NIC is a hardware component that allows a device to connect to a network, either through wired or wireless communication.

🧠 Conclusion

A network is a vital system that connects devices to share information, resources, and services efficiently. It enables communication through technologies like wired and wireless connections, making activities such as browsing, file sharing, and online collaboration possible. From small local networks to the global Internet, networks play a crucial role in modern life. They improve productivity, speed, and accessibility while supporting businesses, education, and entertainment. As technology advances, networks continue to become faster, more secure, and more reliable, forming the backbone of the digital world and connecting people and devices everywhere.
